The Gaps
What the proof of concept does not cover yet
We are transparent about two open items: historical case learning (the voting model you asked for) and the audit trail on sent emails. We explain why they are gaps, how we defended the approach we took, and what the path forward looks like.

How to close the gaps - your decision
Option 1 is what is live today. Option 2 adds Data Cloud for historical learning (Calculated Insights, CRM Connector). Option 3 extends your existing custom LWC to embed the draft trigger and add the audit trail. We walk through what each option requires and recommend a sequence.
