First Contact vs Reply

What you are testing

There are now two purpose-built prompt templates and 10 test cases across three scenario groups. Each prompt has one job with no conflicting responsibilities.

CSR First Contact Email

Use on a new case with no prior email thread. Selects the right template from the library, outputs the full email including the complete document checklist and all submission requirements. Personalises with case-specific details.

CSR Reply Email

Use on an existing case with a reply thread. Reads all EmailMessages on the case, identifies the customer's specific questions, drafts a focused reply. Never repeats the checklist already sent. Answers what was asked — nothing more.

Why two prompts? A single prompt tried to handle both scenarios using conditional instructions. Testing showed that Instruction 4 ("preserve all mandatory template language") and checklist-suppression instructions were irresolvably in conflict — Instruction 4 always won. Two prompts with no shared responsibilities is the correct fix. Each prompt is purpose-built and has no internal contradictions.

How to run each test

  1. Go to the Cases tab in the org → search the case subject from the table above
  2. Open the case → click Activity → click Email to open the compose window
  3. Click Draft with AI → select either CSR First Contact Email or CSR Reply Email depending on the test case → click Generate
  4. Read the output against the checklist in the Path A or Path B tab of this guide
Which prompt to pick: First Contact = new case, no thread. Reply = case already has emails from the customer. When in doubt, check the Activity feed — if there are EmailMessage records on the case, use Reply.

Path A vs Path B — What to Point Out to the Customer

Run the same template scenario on its Path A and Path B case side by side. These pairs prove that the AI adapts to context — same template logic, completely different output based on whether a conversation already exists.

Template A — Medical Director Change

Path A — A-1 — First Contact
Opening: "Thank you for contacting the Allergan Moxie Provider Services team..."

Body: Full document checklist (Form AM-CMD-001, license copy, W-9), complete with submission instructions and portal link.

Tone: Welcoming and thorough — setting expectations for the first time.

What AI added: Dr. Patel named, CA license noted, case number filled.
Path B — B-1 — Follow-up Reply
Opening: "Thank you for your follow-up regarding the Medical Director change..."

Body: Answers the two specific questions — digital signature policy, deadline relative to Dr. Kim's last day. Does not re-list the forms already sent.

Tone: Focused and responsive — acknowledges prior exchange and addresses the gap-in-credentialing concern directly.

What AI added: Advice on submitting forms at least 2 weeks before departure.
Point to the customer: same 1-click process. The AI detected there was an existing conversation and adapted automatically. The CSR does not have to scroll back through the thread and manually decide what to say.

Template B — New Practice Enrollment

Path A — A-2 — First Contact
Opening: "Thank you for your interest in enrolling..."

Body: All 5 required documents listed with form references. Personalised section for Dr. Vasquez, $2M/$4M coverage, Malibu address, CA + NV licenses.

SLA: 10–14 business days from receipt of complete application.

Closing: "We look forward to welcoming you to the provider network."
Path B — B-2 — Follow-up Reply
Opening: "Thank you for your follow-up..."

Body: Directly answers the two questions — can they submit with the old policy while upgrading, and when does the 10-14 day clock start. Confirms what has already been received (4 of 5 docs). Flags only the outstanding item.

Tone: Encouraging — practice is close to completing enrollment.

Template C — Not Eligible

Path A — A-3 — First Contact
Opening: "We regret to inform you..."

Body: States the two eligibility failure reasons from the case description. References Form AM-EL-010. States the 30-day dispute window.

Tone: Empathetic but definitive. First communication of the decision.

Closing: "We remain committed to supporting your practice."

Opening: "Thank you for your follow-up..."

Body: Acknowledges the dispute was received and documents reviewed. Provides a status update on review timeline. Apologises for the delay.

Tone: Supportive and process-oriented — the eligibility decision is no longer final, it is under review.

What AI avoided: Did not re-send the not-eligible determination to a customer who is in the middle of a formal dispute.
This pair is the most compelling for compliance stakeholders. The same template handles both the initial determination AND the dispute follow-up, but produces completely different language — because the AI read the thread and understood the context had changed.

Overall talking point for the customer

Use this framing when you show the comparison:

"A first-year CSR and a five-year CSR both click the same button. The difference is that the five-year CSR reads the full email history before writing. They know what was already said, what questions need answering, and what not to repeat. That is exactly what we built into Path B — the AI reads the thread before drafting, the way an experienced rep would. The mandatory language from your templates is still preserved in both paths. What changes is the intelligence about where the conversation is at."
